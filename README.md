# Infosys ESG Gap Analysis — BRSR 2023-24

An independent ESG gap analysis evaluating Infosys's BRSR disclosure against GRI, TCFD, SEBI BRSR Core, and IFRS S2 standards.

## Covers
- ESG performance scorecard (6 dimensions)
- What Infosys reports well
- 4 material disclosure gaps
- 3 greenwashing risk areas
- 5 strategic recommendations

**Author:** Esha Varyani — MSc Fintech & Business Analytics, University of Westminster
**Data source:** Infosys Integrated Annual Report & ESG Report 2023-24 (public)
